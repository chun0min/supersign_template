Stay Safe:

Public Transportation

Guidelines

Simple actions can help protect yourself and others while commuting.

Hold On for Safety

Hold handrails or straps firmly to prevent falls.Sudden stops or sharp turns can happen at any time. By holding onto a rail or strap, you reduce the risk of losing balance and injuring yourself or others. A steady grip makes everyone’s journey safer.
